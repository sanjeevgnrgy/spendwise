# Merchants and their usual category

Common merchants in India, and the category an expense there usually belongs in. What was bought can override it (see
SKILL.md).

| Merchant (and spellings) | Category |
|---|---|
| Swiggy, Swiggy Instamart, Zomato, Blinkit, Zepto, BigBasket, Dunzo, Domino's, McDonald's, KFC, Starbucks, Chaayos, Café Coffee Day, Third Wave Coffee, Blue Tokai, Haldiram's | Food |
| Ola, Uber, Rapido, Namma Yatri, BluSmart, Namma Metro, Delhi Metro, Mumbai Metro, BEST, BMTC, any "metro" or "auto", Indian Oil, HP Petrol, Bharat Petroleum, FASTag | Transport |
| IndiGo, Air India, Vistara, Akasa Air, SpiceJet, IRCTC (long-distance trains), MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Cleartrip, Yatra, OYO, Taj Hotels, Airbnb, redBus | Travel |
| Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Nykaa, Meesho, Decathlon, IKEA, Croma, Reliance Digital, DMart, Lifestyle, Westside | Shopping |
| BESCOM, Tata Power, Adani Electricity, MSEDCL, BWSSB, Indane, Bharat Gas, Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL, ACT Fibernet, Hathway, LIC, any "electricity", "water board", "broadband" | Bills |
| Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar, JioCinema, SonyLIV, Spotify, YouTube Premium, BookMyShow, PVR INOX, Steam, PlayStation | Entertainment |
| Apollo Pharmacy, MedPlus, 1mg, PharmEasy, Netmeds, Practo, Apollo Hospitals, Fortis, Manipal Hospitals, Dr Lal PathLabs, Thyrocare, Cult.fit | Health |
| Donations, gifts, government fees, bank charges | Other |

Notes:
- IRCTC: a city's suburban train is Transport; a long-distance train is Travel.
- Uber Eats and Ola Foods are Food; Amazon Prime (the video service) is Entertainment; Amazon Pharmacy is Health.
- A merchant not listed here: use the rules in SKILL.md.
